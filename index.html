About the "It's Biblical Uploader" app

It's Biblical Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the It's Biblical channel. It uploads the channel's own videos to YouTube and manages their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists, publishing schedule and comments. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy covers the "It's Biblical Uploader" application, a private tool used only by the owner of the It's Biblical YouTube channel (@its.biblical).

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google OAuth, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own videos and to manage their metadata, thumbnails, captions, playlists and comments. It accesses only the channel owner's own YouTube account.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish and manage the channel's own content. OAuth credentials are stored privately in the owner's secured environment and are never shared or sold. The app does not collect personal data from viewers or from any other users, and does not use cookies or analytics on this site.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner is also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

The app stores no YouTube data beyond what is needed to complete each upload or update. Video files, captions and metadata are the channel owner's own content. API data read from YouTube (such as video status or comments on the channel's own videos) is used immediately and is not stored for more than 30 days. To request deletion of any stored data, contact the channel through the link below; it will be deleted within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. When access is revoked, stored credentials stop working and are deleted.
